# 📊 Retail Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

A professional interactive **Power BI dashboard** built using the **Sample Superstore Dataset** to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and product insights.

This project was created to demonstrate business intelligence, data visualization, and analytical reporting skills.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The dashboard helps answer important business questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which categories and regions are the most profitable?
- Which customer segments contribute the most sales?
- How do discounts impact profitability?
- Who are the top customers?

---

# 📄 Dashboard Pages

## Page 1 – Executive Sales Dashboard

Features:

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Total Customers KPI
- Average Discount
- Profit Margin
- Average Order Value
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 10 Revenue-Generating Products
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Region
- Interactive Slicers
  - Year
  - Region
  - Category
  - Segment

---

## Page 2 – Customer & Product Insights Dashboard

Features:

- Sales by Segment
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Profit vs Discount
- Top 10 Customers by Sales
- Business Insights
- Business Recommendations

---

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest profit.
- West region recorded the highest profitability.
- Consumer segment contributed the highest sales.
- Sales showed a steady growth trend from 2014 to 2017.
- A small number of products generated a significant share of total revenue.
- Furniture generated the lowest profit among all categories.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for top-selling products.
- Focus marketing efforts on Technology products.
- Improve Furniture profitability through pricing optimization.
- Expand successful strategies from the West region.
- Review discount policies to improve profit margins.
